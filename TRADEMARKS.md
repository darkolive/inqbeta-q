# Names and marks

Q's code is free to use under its licences. **Its names are not.** A licence
for code does not include the right to use anyone's name or marks.

Dark Olive CIC uses these names and marks for Q and itself:

- **inQbeta**
- **Q**, in its mark (the Q logo)
- **Dark Olive**, and the Dark Olive logo

## You may

- Say that your work is **based on Q** or **uses Q**, or **works with inQbeta**,
  in plain text.
- Refer to Q by name when writing about it, teaching it, or reviewing it.
- Run the unchanged, published Q and call it Q.

## Please do not

- Call a **changed** version Q or inQbeta, or use the Q mark for it. Give your
  version its own name and its own mark.
- Suggest that Dark Olive CIC made, checked or endorses your version or
  service.
- Sell credits, hosting or support **as** "Q" or "inQbeta". Selling your own
  service built on Q, under your own name, is welcome.

If in doubt, ask. Permission is given gladly to community and non-commercial
projects that keep people's data theirs.
