# Handover — 3 October 2026

A long day (2 October, into the small hours). Everything below is in the
repo; ADRs are in `docs/decisions/` and the project.

## What was built

**Hosts, services and the node**
- Install local first (ADR-Q-018): `/setup` cards make the host on the
  founder's own computer; Services hold settings in `.env`, each with a
  signed public record (`static/host/services.json`); keys are sent to Vercel
  from the page.
- The node runs four jobs, all Reached on localhost and live: **bellboy**
  (Mosquitto), **directory** (Dgraph), **storage** (SeaweedFS + the gate),
  **switchboard** (coturn). Capacity estimates: `q/node-capacity.md`,
  `q/node-sizes.md`, `node/bin/capacity.mjs`.
- Storage takes posts up to **2 MB**; publishes its free allowance at
  `GET /terms`: **25 MB held per inbox**, **50 MB sent per person per day**
  (env `GATE_INBOX_HOLDS_MB`, `GATE_SEND_MB_PER_DAY`).

**Federations**
- Federations are a plugin the host switches on (ADR-Q-020); the club page
  (Home portal, Members, Communication, Services, Settings) with intro slides.
- **FederationStory**: a six-scene picture story on the Federations page.

**Communication**
- **MessageStory** on Messages; **CallStory** on Video call.
- Video call home: Find someone (searches the address book), Your calls, and
  a Camera and sound tab. **The camera is only asked for on that tab or when
  calling.**
- **Voice messages** (ADR-Q-022 Part A): leave one when a call isn't
  answered; 48 kbps Opus (AAC on Safari), up to 2 minutes; sealed, collected,
  kept by both; shown in the conversation and Your calls.
- **A call that has ended never rings late**: the caller sends `call-ended`;
  the voice message replaces the missed call.
- The AI receptionist is designed, not built (ADR-Q-022 Part B): business
  cards only, the caller told first.

**The commons and credits**
- Messages are **compressed before sealing** (`sealTo(…, { zip: true })`):
  voice messages about a quarter smaller.
- **Your use this month** on the home page, from your own receipts.
- **Credits** (ADR-Q-023): buy, spend, reward and trade as receipts, each
  **inspected by Cedar** before it's kept (`q-actions/src/core/credits.ts`);
  treaties as derived rules that can only tighten; balances added up from the
  vault (`q-core/src/credits.ts`); the **Credits** page with a test-mode pack
  (10, Standard, no money taken).
- ADR-Q-017 addendum: the free tier is for passing things between people;
  sustained by use, not investors; clubs cover their own resources through
  their own terms.

**Decisions written**: ADR-Q-018 to ADR-Q-023.
