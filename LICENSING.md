## In plain words

- **Use it, run it, change it, share it.** All of it, for any purpose.
- **The app stays open.** If you change Q and let people use your changed
  version — including over a network, as a website — you must offer them your
  changed source under the same licence. Nobody can take Q and close it.
- **The format is for everyone.** The libraries that read and write Q's
  receipts, keys and actions are under Apache 2.0, so any project — open or
  not — can speak Q's format without adopting the app's licence.
- **The name is not included.** A changed Q must carry its own name. See
  [`TRADEMARKS.md`](TRADEMARKS.md).

Free to give — never free to take.
